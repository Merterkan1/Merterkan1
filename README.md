## Hi, I'm Mert 👋

I'm a data scientist focused on **machine learning for music and audio** — currently completing an MSc in Sound and Music Computing at **Queen Mary University of London**, with an industry-partnered thesis on time-series anomaly and trend detection in streaming data.

Before this, I built end-to-end ML pipelines and BI tooling for the music industry (Kahuna, Havas CX, UNIS Academy) and completed a BSc in Data Science at Eindhoven University of Technology / Tilburg University.

### What I work on

- **MIR & audio deep learning** — beat tracking, music tagging, audio fingerprinting, source identification.
- **Time-series ML** — anomaly detection and forecasting on multi-platform streaming and social data (Spotify, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).
- **Tooling & integration** — text-to-SQL, real-time analytics platforms, custom training pipelines.

| Repo | What it is |
